Just Drop It Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026
Applies to Just Drop It 2.11 and later and The Antipodal Project on Android and iOS

1. Summary

Just Drop It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game works offline, and everything it remembers stays on your device.

The game does not connect to any server run by us. Purchases of optional level packs are handled entirely by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS).

2. Children

Just Drop It is made for players of all ages, including children. Because the game collects no personal information on our servers, we have no player information to share, sell or use for advertising. The local-only design and grown-up checks help parents control purchases and links without creating an account for a child.

A grown-up check (a multiplication question in words) comes before anything that spends money, opens the app store's code-redeeming page, opens a link to an outside website, or shares a picture with another app. Nothing about the answer is stored or sent.

The game has no chat, no friends, no public profiles, no user-made content and no way for players to contact each other.

3. What the game stores on your device

The game saves these things locally, in the app's own storage, so it can remember your progress and settings. None of it is sent anywhere by the game:

Progress and scores
Which World Tour stops you have cleared, your best score, damage percentage and stars on each planet, your top scores for each planet (up to 10 per planet, with the date and time), banked points, and small hints such as which tips you have already seen.
Player name (optional)
Settings has a box where you can type a name for the scoreboard, up to 16 characters ("You" if you never type one). It is only shown on your own device, so a nickname works fine.
Settings
Sound, music and speech on or off, volume, vibration, the graphics setting and whether the title-screen demo is on.
Which packs you own
A copy of the list of level packs that the app store says you own, so they work offline. Each time the game starts it checks the list again with the store.
Level cache
Prepared building shapes that let planets load faster. This is game data only.

To delete all of it: use "Reset game" in Settings, or uninstall the game, or clear its data. On Android: Settings > Apps > Just Drop It > Storage > Clear data (menu names vary a little by device). On iOS: delete the app. Reset game keeps your purchases, because the app store holds those. Copies in a device backup (section 8) are separate.

4. What we collect

Nothing. Specifically:

  • No name, email address, password, phone number or address. There are no accounts.
  • No ads and no advertising identifier. The game contains no advertising software.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.
  • No device identifiers sent to us. We receive nothing from the game at all.

All of the game's levels, pictures and sounds come bundled inside the app. The game makes no network requests to us. The only things that can use the internet are the app store's purchase features and the grown-up-checked links in sections 5 and 6.

5. Purchases and codes

Level packs are optional, one-time purchases. The game itself is free to download and the World Tour is free to play. Purchases are made through Google Play (using Google Play Billing) on Android or the App Store on iOS. The store handles your payment, and we never see card details, your name or your email address. All we learn, on your device, is which packs the store says you own. The store's own privacy policy governs what it keeps.

If you have a promo code, "Redeem a code" (after the grown-up check) opens Google Play's code page, or on iOS Apple's code sheet. The code goes to the store, not to us.

6. Links and sharing

After the grown-up check, a few planets' help screens can open a link to an outside reference website in your device's browser, and a Share button on the results card can hand a picture and a short message to an app you pick, using your device's share sheet. Those websites and apps are not ours and have their own privacy policies; this policy does not cover them. The game sends nothing to them except the picture and message you choose to share.

7. Sound and optional narration

The game can give your device's speech engine fixed foreman phrases and the authored Discovery story for optional narration. It never gives that engine your player name or anything you type. The selected speech service and voice are governed by your device maker's settings and privacy terms; some voices may need an internet connection. You can turn speech off in Settings and narration off in Discovery.

Music and sound effects, including sounds created with ElevenLabs during development, are bundled audio files. Playing them does not send any information to ElevenLabs or contact its services.

8. Device backups

Your phone's own backup system may copy the game's on-device data (everything in section 3) to your personal backup. On Android that is Auto Backup to your own Google account; on iOS it is an iCloud or computer backup, if you have those turned on. That copy belongs to you and is governed by Google's or Apple's terms. The developer cannot see or access it. You can turn backups off or delete them in your device or account settings.

9. Permissions

On Android the game asks for permission to use the internet and to check the network state (needed by Google Play purchases and by the links above), to use Google Play Billing, and to vibrate the phone. It asks for nothing else. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

10. Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct, export or delete. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about your child's use of the game, or about this policy, please contact us using the details below.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top will change too. If a later version of the game adds any online feature or collects any information, this policy will be updated first, and the stores' privacy declarations will be updated to match. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
